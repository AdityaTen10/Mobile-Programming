Main Objective:
To develop a mobile-based digital queue management system that helps citizens book tokens, check government service requirements, and track their queue status without waiting for long periods at government offices.

Specific Objectives:

To develop a digital token booking system that allows citizens to reserve a queue number before visiting the government office.
To provide information about government services and clearly display the required documents and procedures for each service.
To reduce physical waiting time and overcrowding at government offices through digital queue management.
To allow citizens to track their queue status and receive notifications when their turn is approaching.
To provide an administrative system through which government staff can manage tokens, queues, appointments, and completed services.
To provide estimated waiting times so citizens can plan their visit more effectively.
To maintain digital records of service requests and queue activities for better management and reporting.
To improve the overall accessibility and convenience of local government services through a simple and user-friendly mobile application.
